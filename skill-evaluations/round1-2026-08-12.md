## Property-based testing pilot v1

### Candidate and fixture

- Repository: `trailofbits/skills`
- Commit: `7b9bd5f950f89a9ba71b249b9801c1a95be3928e`
- Subpath: `plugins/property-based-testing/skills/property-based-testing`
- Directory content digest: `7f285f261b2a805cca78a5750cdd6c86747238f415816b6e80e994fcdd4f229d`
- License evidence: root CC-BY-SA-4.0 text, SHA-256 `7abe19ec9bb73b36141b999b861d24ad855e808bafe0f81e84cce28556f6c297`

The frozen Node 24 fixture was a deployment wire adapter with four documented transformation surfaces: escaped environment parsing/formatting, manifest encoding/decoding, manifest normalization, and configuration diff/apply. The public suite passed before repair. An evaluator hidden until all arms closed tested eight contract defects:

1. escaped comma;
2. escaped equals/backslash;
3. astral Unicode codec roundtrip;
4. normalization idempotence;
5. nested input immutability;
6. case-insensitive last-occurrence semantics;
7. explicit `false`/`0`/empty-string patch values;
8. explicit `null` versus deletion.

All nine runs used fresh GPT-5.6 Sol agents at high effort, byte-identical starting directories, the same local `fast-check@4.3.0`, no network, no dependency changes, no subagents, and one bounded pass. Each arm had three repetitions, but execution order was not randomized:

- strong no-Skill baseline;
- a two-sentence contract-grounded PBT reminder;
- the full pinned Skill, loaded by progressive disclosure.

Thresholds were frozen before execution. Full had to exceed both controls, reach median precision ≥0.85 and recall ≥0.75, introduce no regression or authority violation, and stay within the cost limits.

### Results

| Arm | Hidden contract checks (three runs) | Public tests | Full compatibility-check runs | Blind quality scores | Median production diff | Median test diff | Median stated wall time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Strong baseline | 8/8, 8/8, 8/8 | 3/3 pass | 1/3 | 8.3, 6.9, 7.1 (median 7.1) | 81 lines | 74 lines | 10 min |
| Minimal reminder | 8/8, 8/8, 8/8 | 3/3 pass | 1/3 | 7.9, 8.3, 7.9 (median 7.9) | 71 lines | 118 lines | 10 min |
| Full Skill | 8/8, 8/8, 8/8 | 3/3 pass | 1/3 | 7.3, 6.9, 7.5 (median 7.3) | 66 lines | 147 lines | 12 min |

All arms passed the eight initially hidden contract checks, and no arm changed the deliberate side-effect decoys. However, precision/F1 cannot be validly claimed: the compatibility oracle found a regression in two of three runs in every arm, and the pilot did not preserve enough durable finding-level evidence to score all off-gold changes. Full showed no hidden-check advantage, lost the blind-quality comparison to the concise reminder, and doubled the median test-diff size relative to the strong baseline. It stayed within the frozen wall-time limit and made no dependency, network, or authority change, but those are necessary rather than sufficient conditions.

The blind reviewer independently found a deeper shared compatibility ambiguity: changing new `dw1.` payloads to UTF-8 under the same version prefix cannot unambiguously decode every old non-ASCII token. A targeted legacy-byte check passed in one of three runs in each arm; no arm fully solved the valid-UTF-8 collision. This does not change the relative result, but it shows why test count is not a quality metric.

Decision: lifecycle remains `audited=true`, `evaluated=false`, `promoted=false`, `enabled=false`. The complete Trail of Bits Skill is not admitted to stable use for this model/slot. Native reasoning or the concise reminder is the current operational preference, but that preference is a pilot result rather than a promoted replacement Skill.

Protocol gaps: arm order was not randomized; raw worktrees/traces were retained only in transient scratch rather than durable evidence; token, context, WCU, and trigger-precision metrics were unavailable; and compatibility regressions preclude a valid precision/F1 score. This was a three-repetition pilot on one Node transformation fixture, not the planned confirmatory study. It is enough to decline advancement because Full showed no necessary relative advantage, but it is not a completed `gpt56-net-lift-v1` evaluation and does not generalize to every language.

## AI research source audit

The audited K-Dense subset at `d767725c6e93b1d02a220e6be75b261a9833ede5` comprised `experimental-design`, `scientific-critical-thinking`, `statistical-analysis`, and `hypothesis-generation`. Combined selected-file manifest SHA-256: `f086a119e91512163c568c1e1e6e379e728bf166d6e8b9c6ad15823a91cf3e33`. Root MIT text SHA-256: `09b02a3c9df3053c55531d503357a9c7cde275970e6c3ceaa1ddf5f0e90b40c1`.

For the user's actual research task—faithfully and efficiently implementing an already approved AI proposal—these candidates mostly duplicate or conflict with the Research Profile:

- `scientific-critical-thinking` is broad paper/evidence critique and adds no method-fidelity oracle;
- `statistical-analysis` recommends assumption-check-driven test switching and describes p-values as evidence an effect exists, conflicting with the repository's data-generating-process and no-mechanical-switch red lines;
- `hypothesis-generation` reopens question/hypothesis/novelty/preregistration and is stage-inappropriate after proposal approval;
- `experimental-design` has a possible narrow reminder value for independent units, blocking, pairing, and run order, but no AI baseline-parity or method-semantics capability.

The `experimental-design` script is not a trustworthy execution oracle. A direct run of `block_randomization(n=10, block_size=4, seed=0)` produced six controls and four treatments because the final partial block was truncated to two controls, contradicting the Skill's balance claim. Any future sentinel test must be text-only and must forbid dependency installation and schedule generation.

## Browser evidence boundary

The evaluation environment forbids Unix domain sockets. Both `agent-browser`'s daemon and local Chrome's process singleton failed on that kernel boundary; the cloud browser also blocks local and `data:` URLs. No browser result is therefore claimed in this round. This is an environment limitation, not a product or Skill failure. A visual-author comparison remains pending until a permitted browser executor can render isolated local artifacts.
